## Project overview

The **Design Your Future** competition by CJ Corporation challenged participants to create innovative product solutions. My approach went beyond conceptual design, focusing on a robust **UX/UI System Design** that bridges the gap between Figma mockups and production-ready code.

## Design thinking & System design

A core focus of this project was establishing a comprehensive design system that serves as a single source of truth for both designers and developers.

### Design Goal & Concept

The conceptual foundation of the CJ Crop project aligns with the core theme **"Design Your Future"**. We defined two central pillars to guide the creative process:

*   **Vibrant & Compelling First Impression**: Establish a high-fidelity visual entrance for K-Festa 2026 that immediately captures the scale, energy, and excitement of the event.
*   **Intuitive Information Delivery**: Create an accessible, structured schedule and content layout for the festival's three thematic weeks: **Food**, **Sports**, and **Movie**.

By anchoring the project around these twin goals, the design translates the abstract concept of a future-focused festival into concrete interactive elements.

### Key Design Rationale

To bring this digital space to life, the interface emphasizes lighting, dimension, and meaning:

*   **Depth and Motion**: I aim to create a design space filled with depth and motion — a place where viewers can “see the future” in every frame. By combining design techniques in lighting, space, and color, the work goes beyond visual expression to evoke emotional experiences, allowing users to feel the spirit of the festival even before they take part.
*   **Color Harmony**: The color palette is deliberately refined and balanced to capture the harmony between creative energy and modern aesthetics, shaping a “future within reach.”
*   **Metaphor of the Brush**: I believe that on the journey of “Design Your Future,” the users are the artists of their own future, while CJ Group serves as the brush — providing the tools, inspiration, and foundation for everyone to design their own path forward.
*   **Spatial Techniques**: Throughout the design process, I focused on creating a sense of depth and spatial dimension within the interface by combining techniques involving shadows and blur effects. Specifically, I used Shadow and the Smooth Shadow plugin to produce soft, layered shadows that make images and components appear more three-dimensional and realistic.

### Key User Scenario

The interface layout is designed to prioritize key user pathways:

> **User Journey: Exploring the Food Festival**
>
> *   **User Profile**: **Tri**, a university student who likes Korean cuisine.
> *   **Entry**: Accesses the landing page to attend the food event.
> *   **Immediate Value**: Upon opening the page, he will see the organizer, event information, and immediate discounts and offers.
> *   **Action**: He will then click on the event representative character he is interested in or scroll down to read the detailed event information right away.

The project was recognized in the **Top 5 Excellent Projects** out of numerous submissions. By combining solid design thinking with technical execution, I demonstrated how a well-structured system design can elevate a product from a mere concept to a practical, working solution.
