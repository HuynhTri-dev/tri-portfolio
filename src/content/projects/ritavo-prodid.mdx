## Project overview

ProdID is an internal Odoo 18 module for managing the product lifecycle from procurement or manufacturing through marketing, approval and commercial readiness.

It creates one traceable workflow across **R&D, Marketing, Sales and Purchasing**, reducing the gap between product data and the teams responsible for bringing a product to market.

## My role

As a Software Engineer and Odoo Developer at Rita Võ, I translated business requirements into modules, data rules and delivery automation:

- Built product management and dynamic attribute capabilities.
- Implemented Excel import and export workflows.
- Added approval flows, audit tracking and access control.
- Managed product media and commercial-readiness data.
- Added DFS-based bill-of-materials cycle detection.
- Deployed the system to staging with Docker Compose.
- Created a GitHub Actions pipeline for automated tests and build validation.

## Scale and constraints

The module operated across approximately **10,000 product records**. Data quality and access control were therefore core product requirements rather than secondary technical details.

## Key engineering decision

### Detecting cycles before a BOM becomes invalid

A recursive bill of materials can create non-terminating calculations and unreliable manufacturing data. A depth-first-search check detects cycles while the structure is being validated, preventing invalid relationships from entering the product lifecycle.

## Delivery outcome

The resulting workflow connected multiple business functions in one traceable system and reached a containerized staging environment with continuous build validation.
